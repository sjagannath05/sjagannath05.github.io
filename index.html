Mobile core · Go & C · 14 years

I write the network functions that carry mobile traffic

GTP, Diameter, SS7/SIGTRAN, RADIUS, and the DNS and lifecycle tooling around them. Most of it is the unglamorous middle of a mobile network: routing decisions, failover paths, and the tooling that makes a carrier interconnect survive a bad Tuesday.

Below is what I actually built, with the commit counts behind each claim and a label saying whether I authored it, contributed to it, or only operated it. Where there is a write-up, it is linked. Where a number does not exist, I have not invented one.

Earlier work

Twelve years before any of the above, in defence communications and then 4G/5G product development. None of it has a public repository behind it, so there are no commit counts here — it is listed the way a record should be when the evidence is a shipped system rather than a diff. It ran roughly in that order: instruments, then models, then radio planning, then building the thing.

2012 – 2015

Instruments, models, and the first virtual data centre

Senior engineer, defence communications
  • Network performance and cable testing with JDSU/Viavi and VeEX instruments (MTS 5800, MTS 6000A, TPS, TX300s).
  • Simulated and emulated both wired and wireless networks in OPNET/Riverbed Modeler, CORE Emulator and NS-3 — OSPF and MANET mobile routing models, parametric studies against selected variables, and integration of the simulated environment with the physical one so a mixed topology could be exercised end to end.
  • Cut dynamic-network convergence time by tuning OSPF timers across a 150+ routed network in OPNET.
  • Designed and built a tactical data centre on VMware over ATCA hardware (Radisys and Emerson chassis and blades), exercising DVS, DRS, HA, SRM and FT, plus a Horizon View VDI pilot — then demonstrated it to the customer.
  • Used the same VMware estate to emulate a tactical communications network with virtual routers, as a cheaper stand-in for the physical one.
  • Ported Cisco ESR onto custom hardware — ATCA, Adlink CPCI single-board computers and in-house boards — and built a Windows VoIP softphone on the Ozeki SDK, integrated against Cisco Call Manager and Asterisk.
  • Multi-vendor routing and switching (Cisco Catalyst, Juniper, MikroTik, Kontron, Radisys and Emerson ATCA switch modules); OSPF, EIGRP and VLAN trunking; CCNA/CCNP lab environments in GNS3 and Packet Tracer.
  • Requirement gathering and analysis through to requirement documents, vendor selection, user manuals and quick-start guides, and customer demonstration.
2016 – 2018

Virtualization

Assistant manager, design & engineering
  • Tactical data-centre proof of concept: VMware ESXi on ATCA hardware, exercising DVS, DRS, HA, SRM, FT and VSAN.
  • Emulated vendor network products with virtual routers from Cisco, Juniper and Mikrotik alongside open-source FRR and VyOS.
  • Built a virtualized environment of virtual routers and Asterisk-based voice services, and modelled CAPEX against a 150+ node network of physical routers.
  • Automated VM and virtual-network deployment on VMware with PowerShell and Ansible; evaluated ATCA blade servers for hyper-converged VSAN suitability.
  • Owned the compute subsystem of a CPCI gateway system for a national defence customer — driver recompilation onto legacy operating systems (Fedora 8, Windows Server 2008) and an IP monitoring system covering six OS variants.
2019 – 2022

Solution architecture

Manager, design & engineering
  • Private LTE solutioning for a national defence customer: multi-vendor core and RAN selection with BoM, RoM and cost optimisation, virtualization, RoIP/PTT and mesh radio.
  • RF simulation in ATDI HTZ Warfare — site optimisation, range and throughput for commercial and vehicle-mounted terminals.
  • Owned the technical track of a shipborne TETRA programme end to end (expression of interest, transfer of technology, RFI/RFP responses, bid submission, technical evaluation) — and won it.
  • Led an LTE-based modular “network in a box” with a technical team, and the compute subsystems for FXO/FXS/RoIP/GSM/E1 gateway products.
  • Built the business case for taking 5G RAN development in-house — spectrum, vendor and framework analysis with cost and headcount — and presented it at CxO level; bootstrapped a 5G testbed on an open-source stack and hardware.
  • Integrated open-source cellular stacks on USRP SDR (srsLTE/srsRAN, OpenAirInterface5G, OpenLTE, NextEPC, Open5GS) and commercial LTE with Airspan eNodeBs against Lemko, Polaris and open-source EPCs; LTE testing with Viavi TM500.
  • Demonstrated solutions at customer units and headquarters, and exhibited at DefExpo (from 2014), DefCon, Arttech and Northtech.
2022 – 2024

Radio

Lead software engineer, 4G/5G products
  • Evaluated the Xilinx RFSoC (ZCU670) platform to decide the direction of in-house radio development, acting as the single point of contact between Xilinx and internal teams for board bring-up, demos and reference image builds.
  • Owned domestic and international RFI/RFP responses for the 4G/5G product line, coordinating internal engineering and external stakeholders.
  • Joined through an acquisition partway through, and carried the product work across the merge.

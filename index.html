Core Network Software Engineer

I architect and operate reliable telecom and cloud-native systems spanning LTE/5G core, virtualization, and modern platform automation. I work hands-on across Kubernetes, AWS, and observability to deliver resilient systems.

I write the network functions that carry mobile traffic — GTP, Diameter, SS7/SIGTRAN, RADIUS — and operate the Kubernetes platform they run on. Most of my work is the unglamorous middle of a mobile network: the routing decisions, the failover paths, and the tooling that makes a carrier interconnect survive a bad Tuesday.
